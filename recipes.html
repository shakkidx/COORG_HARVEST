Traditional Coorg Pepper Chicken Fry

A spicy, semi-dry aromatic dish highlighting the deep heat of Coorg Black Pepper, curry leaves, and a dash of local vinegar.

1
Marinate 500g chicken with turmeric, lime juice, and salt for 15 minutes.
2
Heat oil, crackle mustard seeds, and sauté sliced onions, green chilies, and lots of curry leaves until brown.
3
Add the chicken and cook on medium flame. Sprinkle 2 full tablespoons of freshly crushed Coorg Black Pepper.
4
Stir-fry until dry. Garnish with coriander and serve with hot rice rotis (Akki Roti).
15 Mins Coffee Guide

South Indian Filter Coffee Guide

Brew the perfect thick, frothy decoction using our Somwarpet Estate Arabica blend in a traditional metal filter pot.

1
Add 3 tablespoons of Coorg Plantation Arabica Roast powder to the upper compartment of the coffee filter. Press down gently with the plunger.
2
Pour boiling water over the powder until filled. Close lid and let it drip for 10-12 minutes to collect the thick decoction.
3
Boil fresh milk. Pour 2 tablespoons of decoction into a tumbler, add sugar to taste, and pour hot milk from a height to create froth.
4
Serve instantly in a traditional brass cup (Dabara).
10 Mins Hydration

Iced Wild Hibiscus Lemonade

A tart, ruby-red refreshing iced tea filled with Vitamin C, perfect for hot afternoons.

1
Boil 2 cups of water. Turn off heat and add 1 tablespoon of Wild Hibiscus Tea Calyces.
2
Cover and steep for 6-8 minutes. Strain the tea into a pitcher; it will be a dark ruby-red color.
3
Stir in 1 tablespoon of Raw Wild Forest Honey and the juice of half a lime while warm.
4
Allow to cool, add ice cubes, fresh mint leaves, and serve chilled.
5 Mins Immunity

Monsoon Ginger & Honey Immunity Shot

An age-old Kodagu household wellness remedy for soothing sore throats and warding off seasonal colds.

1
Grate 1 inch of fresh ginger root and squeeze out the fresh juice (about 1 teaspoon).
2
Mix the ginger juice with 2 teaspoons of Raw Wild Forest Honey.
3
Add a tiny pinch of ground Coorg Black Pepper.
4
Consume warm in the morning on an empty stomach. Do not drink water for 10 minutes afterwards.
