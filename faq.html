Where are Coorg Harvest products sourced from?

Our products are sourced 100% directly from shade-grown agricultural plantations, private forest estates, and traditional indigenous gatherer collectives in the hills of Kodagu (Coorg), Karnataka, India. Our sorting and packaging unit is located in Gonikopal, Coorg.

Do you support Cash on Delivery (COD)?

Yes, we support Cash on Delivery (COD) for orders shipped to major pin codes across India. You can select Cash on Delivery under our secure payment options on the checkout page.

Is your Forest Honey pasteurized or heated?

Absolutely not. Our Raw Wild Forest Honey is unfiltered and unpasteurized. It is extracted manually from forest combs by indigenous tribes and strained using basic cloth to retain natural enzymes, floral pollen, and minerals. Because it is 100% raw, it may crystallize over time, which is the ultimate seal of honey purity.

How long will it take for my order to be delivered?

We pack and dispatch orders from our Gonikopal hub within 24-48 hours. Shipments to South Indian cities (Bangalore, Chennai, Mysore) usually arrive in 2-3 business days. Deliveries to other parts of India (Mumbai, Delhi, Kolkata, etc.) take between 4-6 business days. A tracking link is automatically shared on your WhatsApp.

What are the shipping charges?

We charge a flat shipping fee of ₹50 for orders below ₹500. For orders of ₹500 and above, shipping is completely FREE across India.

How should I store whole spices like black pepper and green cardamom?

To retain their rich essential oils, spices should be stored in dry, airtight glass jars or opaque steel canisters. Store them in a cool, dark kitchen cupboard away from gas stoves, direct sunlight, and moisture. Avoid using wet spoons to scoop the spices.
